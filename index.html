Why the wording is careful

A 2024 systematic review/meta-analysis found a small laboratory attention benefit from white/pink noise in people with ADHD or high ADHD symptoms, while effects were not beneficial for non-ADHD groups overall. Evidence for brown noise is much more limited. Nature sounds may help relaxation/stress for some people. Background music results are mixed.
