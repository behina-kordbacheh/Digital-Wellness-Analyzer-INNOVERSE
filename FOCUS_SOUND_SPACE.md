# Focus Sound Space

This build adds an optional browser-based Focus Sound Space.

## Sounds included

- White Focus Noise
- Pink Focus Noise
- Deep Brown Noise
- Soft Rain
- Waterfall Flow
- Forest Birds
- Ocean Breathing
- Soft Instrumental Pad

All sounds are procedurally generated in the browser using the Web Audio API.
No copyrighted music or external streaming service is required.

## Personalization

The panel can optionally ask:
- whether the user has ADHD or high ADHD symptoms
- whether they want support for low focus, a busy/overthinking mind, relaxation, or study/work

This context is used only to highlight sound options and is not saved in the local database.

## Scientific wording

The UI does NOT claim that audio treats ADHD, stops overthinking, cures anxiety, or guarantees focus.

Evidence summary used in the product:
- A 2024 systematic review/meta-analysis found a small laboratory attention benefit from white/pink noise in participants with ADHD or high ADHD symptoms, while non-ADHD groups did not show an overall benefit.
- Evidence for brown noise as an ADHD intervention is much more limited.
- Nature sounds may support stress reduction/relaxation for some people, but results vary.
- Background music and cognitive-task performance research is mixed and context-dependent.

## Safety

- Audio starts only after the user clicks Play.
- Default volume is deliberately low.
- The user can pause at any time.
- Auto-stop options: 15, 25, 45, or 60 minutes.
- The UI tells users to stop if the sound feels irritating or distracting and to protect their hearing.
